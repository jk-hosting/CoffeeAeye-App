Privacy policy

Nothing leaves your phone.

Coffee Aeye is a local-only application. It does not collect, transmit, share or sell any user data, images or personal information off the device.

Last updated 17 August 2026

What Coffee Aeye stores

Everything the app knows about you lives in a database file on your own device:

  • Coffee bags you have scanned, including the label photos and the bean profile built from them
  • Recipes, brew sessions, timings, ratings and tasting notes
  • Your grinder setup, preferences and lab measurements

None of this is uploaded. There is no server holding a copy, because there is no server.

No account, no analytics, no tracking

You never create an account or sign in. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifiers and no third-party tracking of any kind. We cannot see how you use the app, and we do not receive usage statistics, device identifiers or crash logs.

This website

Everything above describes the Coffee Aeye app. This website is a separate thing, and it is only fair to be equally specific about it.

coffeeaeye.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is a deliberately minimal, privacy-preserving service:

  • No cookies. Nothing is stored on your device, which is why this site shows you no cookie banner.
  • No fingerprinting and no cross-site tracking. You are not followed from here to anywhere else, and not recognised if you return.
  • No personal data and no individual profiles. What we see is aggregate: how many people visited a page, which page they came from, roughly which country, and whether they were on a phone or a desktop. There is no way for us to identify you or to single out one visitor's activity.
  • No advertising networks and nothing sold or shared with anyone.

Cloudflare processes this on our behalf; their handling is covered by the Cloudflare privacy policy. We use it for one reason: to know which pages are worth writing more of. If you would rather not be counted at all, any content blocker or your browser's Do Not Track setting will stop the script loading.

Links on this site to Google Play carry a campaign tag so we can see which page sent a visit — again a count, not a person. Once you are on Google Play you are subject to Google's privacy policy, not this one.

None of this touches the app. Installing Coffee Aeye gives us nothing, and the app itself remains free of analytics of any kind.

How the AI works

Label reading and the Brew Expert both run entirely on your device. Photographs of your coffee bags are processed locally and are never sent anywhere for analysis.

The one time Coffee Aeye needs the internet is when it downloads its AI model during setup. That download is fetched from Hugging Face, a public file host. It is an ordinary file download — nothing about you or your coffee is sent with it — though, as with any download, the host will see the standard request information such as your IP address. Their handling of that is covered by Hugging Face's privacy policy. After the model is downloaded, the app works with no connection at all.

Backups you create

The backup export writes an archive of your data to a file you choose what to do with. Once you share or copy that file, it is outside the app and its privacy depends on wherever you send it. We never receive it.

Purchases

Coffee Aeye is sold through Google Play. Payment, billing and refunds are handled entirely by Google under Google's privacy policy. We receive only the aggregate sales figures that Play reports to any developer, and never your payment details.

Children

Coffee Aeye is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because no personal data ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase on your behalf. You hold the only copy. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage from Android's settings, removes that data permanently — so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that feature ships, and the date at the top will change with it.
